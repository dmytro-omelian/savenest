SaveNest 0.11.0 — Free on your Mac

  • Every local feature is free. Save and edit without an account, subscription, or network connection.
  • Use the unified ⌘O launcher to search open tabs, saved links, notes, tags, and recent browser history.
  • Review anonymous usage reporting in the launcher or Settings. Turn it off anytime and read the privacy policy.
  • Cloud backup and sync are planned, not yet available. Your library stays on your Mac.

Quit older copies of SaveNest, then replace SaveNest in Applications. Your existing library, settings, and Keychain account remain in use. macOS may ask you to grant browser access to the new application. Export a local backup before upgrading.

This is the first Electron release. Its update feed is separate from the legacy native application's feed. Use SaveNest → Check & Download Updates for future Electron updates.
